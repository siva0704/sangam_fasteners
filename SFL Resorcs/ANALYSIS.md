**Key Takeaways for Website/Content Creation:**
1. **Highlight the Niche**: Focus heavily on "Precision Shafts" rather than general nuts & bolts.
2. **Emphasize Scale & Tech**: They have massive infrastructure (51+ advanced machines, 20,000m² area, robotics installed in 2022).
3. **Showcase Quality**: They emphasize zero-defects, ISO 9001:2015, and use high-end Japanese (Mitutoyo) and CMM testing equipment.
4. **Use the Pillars**: The short `.docx` files should be verbatim implemented as core values on the Home/About pages.
